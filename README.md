# Startup Viability Analysis
## **Project Status**
This project is currently **in progress**, focusing on the customer segmentation part.
## Brief Description
This project aims to assess the viability of a proposed AI-driven gardening robot startup by conducting comprehensive customer segmentation and market opportunity analysis.

## **Project Structure**

- [**customer-segmentation:**](https://github.com/alexhosp/startup-viability-analysis/tree/main/customer-segmentation) This sub-project focuses on identifying and understanding the target audience for the gardening robot. It utilizes Google Trends, social media, and website data to analyze search interest, extract audience characteristics, and segment potential customers based on shared attributes and preferences.
- [**tam-estimation:**](https://github.com/alexhosp/startup-viability-analysis/tree/main/tam-estimation) This sub-project leverages the customer segments identified in the previous step to estimate the Total Addressable Market (TAM) for the proposed gardening robot. It analyzes each customer segment to determine the potential number of customers and their purchasing power, ultimately providing insights into the overall market opportunity.

## **Project Goals**

- **Identify and Understand the Target Audience:** Through data analysis, uncover distinct customer segments for the proposed prototype and their key characteristics, motivations, and needs.
- **Estimate Market Potential:** Quantify the market opportunity for the gardening robot by estimating the Total Addressable Market (TAM) based on identified customer segments.
- **Inform Product Development and Marketing Strategy:** If the idea is considered viable, utilize the insights gained from customer segmentation and TAM estimation to guide the development of a product that resonates with the target audience and develop effective marketing strategies.

## **Key Deliverables**

- **Well-defined Customer Segments:** A clear understanding of the different groups of potential customers, their characteristics, and their needs.
- **TAM Estimation:** A quantitative assessment of the market size and potential revenue for the proposed gardening robot. This includes estimating the total addressable (TAM) market per customer segment and the total TAM.
- **Actionable Insights:** Recommendations for product development, marketing, and business strategy based on the analysis findings.
