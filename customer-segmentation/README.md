# Customer Segmentation Analysis for AI Gardening Robot Startup Idea
## Brief Description
This customer segmentation analysis investigates the target market for an innovative AI gardening robot, utilizing Google Trends, social media, and web search data. It aims to identify desired features and real-world problems and needs to inform prototype design and define distinct audience segments interested in the prototype, as well as its total addressable market. This will ensure a user-centric prototype and enable the startup to capitalize on market opportunities while reducing the risk of wasted resources.
## Core Question
Who are the target customers for a proposed startup idea?
